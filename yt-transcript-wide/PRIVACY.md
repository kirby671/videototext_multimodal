# Privacy policy

_Clean Transcript & Key Learnings for YouTube_ · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This extension does not collect, store or share any data on servers operated by its developer. It has no analytics,
tracking or advertising.

## What stays on your device

- Your settings, your Anthropic API key, and your transcript Library (transcripts, analyses, translations, trend
  reports and company network) are stored in your browser's extension storage.
- If you turn on **Sync to a folder**, the Library is also written as Markdown files to a folder you choose on your own
  computer.
- **Translate** uses Chrome's built-in translator, which runs on your computer. Chrome may download its translation
  model from Google the first time a language pair is used; the text being translated is not sent anywhere.
- Removing the extension deletes its browser storage. Files in a synced folder stay until you delete them.

## What is sent to a third party, and when

Only when you click **Analyze with Claude**, **Translate with Claude Haiku**, **Write the trend report**, **Update
network** or **Test** (in Settings) does the extension send data to Anthropic's Claude API (`api.anthropic.com`),
using the API key you provided. What is sent: the transcript text (for trend reports and the company network: the
saved analyses and short transcript excerpts), the video's title, channel and description, and your "What matters most to you" instructions.
Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by its own [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

The extension also reads the YouTube pages you open (`www.youtube.com`) to show the transcript panel and to fetch a
video's transcript and publish date.

## Your API key

Your API key is stored only in this browser and is sent only to `api.anthropic.com`. It is never placed in a web
page you visit.

## What the developer receives

Nothing. The developer never receives your API key, transcripts, Library or browsing activity, and does not sell or
transfer user data.
